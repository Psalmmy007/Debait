    Understanding Your Feed

    The flagged percentage is Rage, Fear and Envy headlines divided by all analyzed headlines, including Clean. Headlines that failed analysis and developer evaluations do not count.

    A difficult subject or a genuine distressing quotation is not automatically manipulation. Read the evidence before deciding whether to change the wording.

    Clean means no supported current emotional category was identified. It does not verify a claim. Scores are model judgments, not probabilities that a story is false.
